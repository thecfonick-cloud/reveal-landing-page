How to use it

Four taps. Zero effort.

Designed so anyone in the household can use it on day one — no manual, no mess.

Step 01

Plug in & power on

Place ReVeaL on your countertop, plug it in, and tap the power button. The device wakes up in seconds — quiet, ready, glowing softly.

Step 02

Drop in your food scraps

Lift the lid and add peels, leftovers, coffee grounds, eggshells — anything organic. Close the lid. That's the only chore.

Step 03

Press start, walk away

One tap begins the cycle. Sensors handle moisture, temperature and airflow automatically. No stirring, no smell, no babysitting.

Step 04

Collect nutrient-rich output

In a few hours, open the chamber and scoop out clean, dry, soil-ready material. Use it on plants, in your garden, or share with a neighbour.

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

At ReVeaL, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and subscribe to our founding-member waitlist.

When you express interest in ReVeaL and join our waitlist, we collect the personal information you voluntarily provide to us:

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for purposes related to the launch of ReVeaL, including to:

  • Send you updates, promotional offers, and news about the ReVeaL device.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We respect your trust and value your privacy above all else. Specifically:

4. Your Rights & Choice

You have complete control over your subscription. You can:

  • Opt Out: Unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time by clicking the link at the bottom of our emails.
  • Request Deletion: Ask us to remove your email address from our records completely by contacting us at revealearth.co@gmail.com.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this policy, please reach out to us at revealearth.co@gmail.com.
